## Full Surface Comparison

The Earthwork Tool allows you to compare two surfaces to calculate cut and fill quantities across the entire project area. This is essential for understanding the total earthwork required for your site.

### Steps to Compare Surfaces
1. Select the "Surface Comparison" tab in the Earthwork Tool interface.
2. Choose the existing surface and the proposed surface from the dropdown lists.
3. Click "Compare" to run the analysis.

![Earthwork Tool surface comparison](../../../assets/images/EarthworkTool/EW-SurfaceComparison.gif)
<sub>Note: the version on the image may not reflect the latest version of Earthwork Tool.</sub>

### Output
- The tool displays a summary of total cut and fill volumes.
- Results are shown in your preferred units (m³, yd³, ft³, acre-ft).
- Optionally, generate a table or label output for documentation or reporting.

```yaml
# Example Output Table
| Surface Comparison | Cut Volume (m³) | Fill Volume (m³) |
|-------------------|----------------|------------------|
| Existing vs. Proposed | 1200 | 950 |
```
