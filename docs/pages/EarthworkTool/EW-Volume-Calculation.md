## Total Earthwork Calculation

The Earthwork Tool calculates the total earthwork volume by comparing multiple surfaces (existing, stripping, subgrade, base, proposed, etc.) and summing the cut and fill quantities for the entire project or for defined regions.

### Steps to Calculate Total Earthwork
1. Select the surfaces to include in the calculation (e.g., stripping to subgrade, subgrade to proposed).
2. Choose the regions (or use the full site).
3. Click "Calculate Total Earthwork" to run the analysis.

![Earthwork Tool total earthwork calculation](../../../assets/images/EarthworkTool/EW-TotalEarthwork.gif)
<sub>Note: the version on the image may not reflect the latest version of Earthwork Tool.</sub>

### Output
- The tool displays total cut and fill volumes for the selected surfaces and regions.
- Results are shown in your preferred units.
- Export the results as a table or report for documentation.

## Dynamic Volume Unit Update

You can instantly switch between different volume units (m³, yd³, ft³, acre-ft) for all displayed and reported results.

### Steps to Change Units
1. Click the unit selector dropdown in the Earthwork Tool interface.
2. Choose your preferred unit (m³, yd³, ft³, acre-ft).
3. All results update instantly to reflect the new unit.
