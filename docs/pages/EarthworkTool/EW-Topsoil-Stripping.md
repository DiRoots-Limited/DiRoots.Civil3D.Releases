## Creating a Stripping Topsoil Surface

The Earthwork Tool allows you to create a stripping surface by removing the topsoil layer from the existing surface. This is important for accurate earthwork calculations, as topsoil is often stripped and stockpiled before grading begins.

### Steps to Create a Stripping Surface
1. Select the "Topsoil Stripping" tab in the Earthwork Tool interface.
2. Choose the existing surface as the base.
3. Enter the topsoil thickness to be stripped (e.g., 0.2m or 8in).
4. Click "Create Stripping Surface" to generate the new surface.

### Usage in Calculations
- The stripping surface can be used as the starting point for further earthwork calculations.
- Cut/fill volumes can be calculated between the stripping surface and proposed/subgrade surfaces.
- The tool reports the total volume of stripped topsoil for stockpiling or removal.
