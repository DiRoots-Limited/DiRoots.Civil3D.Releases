## Predefining and Managing Stations

Grading Pro provides flexible options for defining and managing stations along your grading alignment, supporting both individual and range-based station definitions.

### Station Definition Methods
- **Individual stations:** Specify exact station locations for detailed control.
- **Range of stations:** Define a start, end, and interval to automatically generate a series of stations.
- **Mixed:** Combine individual and range-based stations for complex grading scenarios.

### Steps to Manage Stations
1. Select the "Station Management" tab in the Grading Pro interface.
2. Choose your method (individual, range, or mixed).
3. Enter station values or ranges as needed.
4. Review and edit the station list before applying to your grading design.

### Using Stations in Grading Design
- Stations control where sections are placed and how grading is applied along the alignment.
- You can update, add, or remove stations at any time, and the design will update accordingly.
